COURSES OFFERED

Courses Offered
GITA Autonomous College offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to provide students with strong academic knowledge, practical skills, and industry-oriented learning. The courses are structured to help students understand fundamental concepts while also providing opportunities to apply their knowledge through laboratory work, projects, internships, workshops, and technical activities.
Undergraduate Programs
The college offers various undergraduate programs in engineering, technology, computer applications, and other professional fields. Some of the major areas of study include:
* Computer Science and Engineering
* Computer Science and Engineering – Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
* Electrical Engineering
* Electronics and Communication Engineering
* Mechanical Engineering
* Civil Engineering
* Computer Applications
* Business and Management-related programs
These programs focus on developing technical knowledge, problem-solving abilities, programming skills, analytical thinking, and practical experience required for professional careers.
Postgraduate Programs
GITA Autonomous College also provides postgraduate education in selected technical and professional disciplines. These programs allow students to gain advanced knowledge, specialize in their chosen fields, and prepare for careers in industry, research, higher education, and entrepreneurship.
#### Learning and Career Development
The courses combine classroom education with practical learning to give students a better understanding of real-world applications. Students get opportunities to work on academic projects, participate in seminars and workshops, attend training programs, take part in technical competitions, and pursue internships.
The curriculum also encourages students to develop communication skills, teamwork, leadership, creativity, and professional ethics. Through these academic and extracurricular opportunities, GITA Autonomous College aims to prepare students for higher studies and successful careers in a rapidly changing technological environment.
