About The Old-town Cafe

The Old-town Cafe is a heritage-inspired cafe that brings together the charm of traditional surroundings and the comfort of modern cafe culture. Our cafe is inspired by the historic character, architecture and lifestyle of Old Town, Bhubaneswar.

We offer delicious food, refreshing beverages and a warm atmosphere where friends, families, students and visitors can relax and spend quality time together. From a peaceful cup of coffee to a satisfying meal, we aim to make every moment special.

The Spirit of Old Town

Old Town is one of the historic parts of Bhubaneswar, known for its traditional architecture, ancient temples, artistic craftsmanship and rich cultural atmosphere. The streets and surroundings reflect a connection between the city's heritage and its modern everyday life.


Inspired by this unique character, The Old-town Cafe brings a small part of that timeless atmosphere into a comfortable modern cafe. Our goal is to create a place where people can enjoy good food, meaningful conversations and the warm spirit of Old Town.
